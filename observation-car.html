Observation Car

a scrapbook for train travel — the route, the views, the whole trip

A scrapbook app for train travel — log the route, the stops, and the moments along the way, and keep the whole trip in one place instead of scattered across a camera roll. Named for the glass-walled lounge car where the best of a train ride tends to happen.
